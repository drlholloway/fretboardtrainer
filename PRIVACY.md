# Fretman privacy policy

Effective September 22, 2026 (updated for stats).

Fretman (Fretboard Trainer) is a guitar and bass fretboard trainer for iOS,
Android, macOS and Linux. It does not collect, send or share any personal
data.

## What the app stores

Everything stays on your device, in the app's own storage:

- **Settings:** instrument, string count, tuning, sharps or flats,
  left-handed view, theme, lesson unlocking and the free-drill options.
- **Lesson progress:** for each lesson, whether you have passed it, how
  many attempts you have made and your best score.
- **Practice stats:** for each fret position and chord, per tuning, how
  many times it was asked, how many answers were right, the average time to
  a right answer, when it was last asked and whether the latest few answers
  were right; plus your day streak and best run of right answers. They
  drive the Stats tab and which questions come up more often.
- **The last launch splash shown,** so the next launch picks a different
  one.

Nothing else is recorded: no names, no email addresses, no location, no
contacts, no microphone or camera, and no device identifiers.

## What the app sends

Nothing. Fretman has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The release builds do
not request network access.

The one link in the app, **Buy me a coffee** in Settings, opens
buymeacoffee.com in your web browser. What happens on that site is covered
by its own privacy policy, not this one.

## Backups

Your phone or computer may include the app's settings and progress in its
own backups (for example iCloud backup on iOS or Android's automatic backup
to your Google account). Those backups are made by the operating system
under your account's settings; Fretman does not see or control them.

## Deleting your data

**Settings → Reset progress** clears your lesson progress and
**Settings → Reset stats** clears the practice stats. Uninstalling the app
deletes everything it stored.

## Children

Fretman collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a
